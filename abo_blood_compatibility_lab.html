Important distinction

This tool describes the usual compatibility rules for packed red blood cells. Plasma and platelet compatibility are different. Real transfusions require laboratory testing, including antibody screening and crossmatching, and must be decided by qualified healthcare professionals.

Red-cell donor guide

Common packed red-cell compatibility, including Rh(D):

Blood groupCan donate red cells toCan receive red cells from
“−” means RhD-negative; “+” means RhD-positive. This is a learning guide, not authorization to transfuse.
O−

No A/B or RhD antigens on red cells; broadest common RBC donor.

AB+

Has A, B and RhD antigens; broadest common RBC recipient.

A / B

ABO compatibility depends on the A or B antigen.

Rh factor

RhD-negative recipients generally should receive RhD-negative red cells.
